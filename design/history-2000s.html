    Jersey City Music in the 2000s

    59 documented entries, every one cited. Listed in order of era, then name. Never ranked.

    Every artist, group, DJ, label and place in The Jersey City Sound documented as active in Jersey City in the 2000s, listed in order of era and name with a cited entry for each. This is one page of the archive's timeline; the full sequence from the 1960s to today is on the History page.

    An entry appears here when its documented years active, an era row on its record card, or a dated event in its sourced facts (a release, a residency, a battle) falls within the period. Birth and death dates do not count as activity. Entries with no dated activity in their sources are not placed in an era until a date is sourced. The archive documents; it does not rank.

    Questions, answered§

    How does the archive decide which era an entry belongs to?

    By the years active recorded in the entry, or failing that by dated events in its sourced facts. An artist active across several decades appears on each decade's page; birth and death dates never place an entry.

    Where is the full Jersey City music timeline?

    On the History page, which links every era page in sequence, and on the Chilltown page for the nickname's own history.
