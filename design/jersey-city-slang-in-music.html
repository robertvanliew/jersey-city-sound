    Jersey City Slang in Music: 201 and Chilltown

    42 documented entries, every one cited. Listed in order of era, then name. Never ranked.

    Two local words run through Jersey City's music record. 201 is the city's area code, worn in DJ handles and crew names and attached to the 201 style of doubled drums and stacked blends that the city's mixtape DJs made their own. Chilltown is the city's hip-hop nickname, in use since the early 1980s. This page lists every entry whose sourced text uses either word.

    The 201 style is named and defined in the 2006 Jersey City DJ Documentary as a Jersey City production signature: blend-driven mixtapes, doubled drums and stacked acapella blends. Benito is documented as a practitioner. The number also marks people and crews: DJ handles such as @djdyce201, @strongvic201, @juggs201 and dj-flamez-201, the New Jersey Mix Kings 201 collective, flyers billing "201 Squad's and Jersey City's Finest," CF201's stage name, and Mista Quietman's The 201 High School Anthem.

    Chilltown has its own page with the documented origin: by the account of pioneer DJ Wimpy Bee, Jersey City kids flipped a line from Ronnie Gee's 1980 record Rap-tivity into Chilltown, Jersey City. The word turns up across the record as the name of the scene, from the founding generation of DJ Stranger Dee and DJ Thurmie Thurm to the Chill Town J.C. Hip-Hop Documentary. See why Jersey City is called Chilltown.

    Questions, answered§

    What does 201 mean in Jersey City music?

    201 is Jersey City's telephone area code, used as shorthand for the city in DJ handles, crew names and song titles. The 201 style is the local mixtape production signature of doubled drums and stacked acapella blends, named in the 2006 Jersey City DJ Documentary.

    What is the 201 style?

    A Jersey City mixtape and production style built on doubled drums and stacked acapella blends, named and defined in The Jersey City DJ Documentary Vol. 1 (2006). Benito is among the producers documented as practicing it.

    Why is Jersey City called Chilltown?

    Chilltown is the city's hip-hop nickname from the early 1980s, when local DJs flipped "Chilltown, New York" from Ronnie Gee's 1980 record Rap-tivity into "Chilltown, Jersey City." The full history is on the Chilltown page.
