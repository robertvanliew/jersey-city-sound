    Music from Greenville, Jersey City

    5 documented entries, every one cited. Listed in order of era, then name. Never ranked.

    Greenville is the southernmost section of Jersey City, and its projects and blocks run through the city's rap record: Curries Woods, Sal-Laf Courts, Bidwell and Ocean, Lexington Avenue. This page gathers every entry in The Jersey City Sound whose sourced text places it in Greenville, with a cited entry for each.

    Max YB represents the Curries Woods projects and the Uptop section and appears alongside DJ Swill B in the city's recent catalog. De$igner Boyz have roots across Curries Woods, Sal-Laf Courts, Bidwell and Ocean, and Lexington Avenue, with a catalog produced largely by Uncle YG and Herogawd. PressureOnline, an artist and media figure who operates pressureonline.com, also represents Curries Woods. The lyricist Harold Adamson, born in Greenville in 1906 and raised in Brooklyn, is the neighborhood's earliest documented name.

    An entry appears here when its own facts or record card name Greenville or a place within it. The archive documents; it does not rank.

    Questions, answered§

    Which rappers are from Greenville, Jersey City?

    In the archive so far: Max YB (Curries Woods, Uptop), De$igner Boyz (Curries Woods, Sal-Laf Courts, Bidwell and Ocean, Lexington Avenue) and PressureOnline (Curries Woods). Others are added as their entries are sourced.

    Is Curries Woods in Greenville?

    Yes. Curries Woods is a public housing complex in the Greenville section of Jersey City, and several entries name it as home.
